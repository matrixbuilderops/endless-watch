## Endless Watch — TV Time, but better

**Status:** Live (public PWA)

A local-first media tracker that auto-logs what you watch across 10 streaming services and keeps your library yours, forever. No subscription, no cloud lock-in, works offline.

### What's new
- Automatic scrobbling across Netflix, Prime, Disney+, Crunchyroll, Hulu, Paramount+, Apple TV+, Max, Peacock, and YouTube
- One-tap import from a TV Time export or Netflix activity file
